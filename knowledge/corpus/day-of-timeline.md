# Building the day-of wedding timeline

The wedding-day timeline is the minute-by-minute schedule that keeps the
celebration flowing and every vendor aligned. Build it backward from the
ceremony start time, then forward through the reception.

Start with hair and makeup, allowing enough time for the whole party plus a
buffer, because beauty appointments are the most common source of delay. Add time
for getting dressed, first-look or pre-ceremony portraits, and travel between
locations. Keep the ceremony itself short, commonly twenty to thirty minutes for
a non-religious service.

After the ceremony, plan for a cocktail hour while the couple and wedding party
finish portraits, then the reception entrance, welcome toast, dinner service,
speeches, first dances, cake cutting, and open dancing. Share the finished
timeline with the venue, caterer, photographer, and band or DJ so everyone hits
the same cues. Always pad transitions with a few extra minutes; a schedule with
no slack falls apart the moment one segment runs long.
