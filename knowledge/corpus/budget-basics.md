# Wedding budget basics

A wedding budget is easiest to manage when you split the total into category
allocations before you book anything. A common starting split is roughly 40% to
the venue and catering combined, 10% to photography and video, 10% to attire and
beauty, 10% to flowers and decor, 10% to music and entertainment, 10% to
stationery, favors, and rentals, and the remaining 10% held back as a
contingency buffer.

Decide your total first, then work backward into the categories. The single
biggest line item for most couples is the venue plus food and drink, so lock a
realistic number there before committing elsewhere. Track every deposit and
balance due date in one place so you always know how much of the budget is
already committed versus still available.

Keep a contingency of about 5% to 10% of the total for surprises: overtime fees,
extra guests, delivery charges, gratuities, and taxes that are quoted separately.
Building the buffer in from the start is far less stressful than trying to find
the money later.
