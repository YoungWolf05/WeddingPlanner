# Managing the guest list and RSVPs

The guest list drives cost more than almost any other choice, because catering,
rentals, favors, and stationery all scale with headcount. Build the list in
tiers: must-invite (immediate family and closest friends), then extended family
and friends, then a wider circle you add only if the budget and venue capacity
allow. Setting tiers up front makes trimming far easier if the numbers run high.

Send save-the-dates about six months ahead and invitations six to eight weeks
before the wedding, with an RSVP deadline about three to four weeks out. That
gives you time to follow up with anyone who has not replied and still deliver a
final headcount to the caterer on schedule.

Expect a portion of invited guests to decline; the actual attending number is
usually lower than the invited number, though the rate varies with travel
distance and timing. Track responses, meal choices, and plus-ones in a single
list, and always plan to personally contact stragglers rather than assuming a
non-reply means no.
