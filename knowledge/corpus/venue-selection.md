# Choosing a wedding venue

The venue shapes almost every other decision, from budget and guest count to
theme and timeline, so choose it early. Start with capacity: the space must
comfortably seat your expected headcount with room for dining, a dance floor,
and any staging.

Compare what each venue includes. Some are all-inclusive with in-house catering,
tables, chairs, linens, and coordination, while others are blank-canvas spaces
where you rent everything and bring outside vendors. All-inclusive venues are
simpler; blank-canvas venues offer more control but require more coordination and
often more budget for rentals.

Ask about the essentials that hide in the contract: the hours included and
overtime rates, whether there is a rain plan for outdoor spaces, parking and
accessibility, noise curfews, vendor restrictions, and what deposit and
cancellation terms apply. Visit at the same time of day and season as your event
if you can, so you see the real light and temperature. Confirm the date is
available and get every promise in writing before you pay a deposit.
