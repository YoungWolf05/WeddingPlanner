# Choosing and booking wedding vendors

Vendors make or break the day, so choose them carefully and book the busiest ones
first. Photographers, caterers, and bands or DJs often reserve popular dates a
year ahead, so secure them soon after the venue. Florists, officiants, and cake
makers usually have more availability but still benefit from early booking.

Vet each vendor before signing. Review a full portfolio or a complete event, not
just highlights, read recent reviews, and ask for references. In a consultation,
confirm they have your date open, understand your style, and can work within your
budget. Ask how they handle emergencies and whether they bring a backup or a
second shooter.

Read every contract closely. Confirm the exact services and hours, the deposit
and payment schedule, overtime rates, the cancellation and refund policy, and who
the actual person on the day will be. Keep all communication and agreements in
writing. A clear contract protects both sides and prevents misunderstandings when
the schedule gets busy.
