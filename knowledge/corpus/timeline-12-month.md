# Twelve-month wedding planning checklist

A twelve-month runway keeps wedding planning calm and orderly. Use it as an
ordered checklist and adjust the pace if your engagement is shorter.

- Twelve months out: set the budget, draft a rough guest count, and choose a
  season or date range. Book the ceremony and reception venues, since popular
  dates go first.
- Nine months out: book the highest-demand vendors, typically the photographer,
  caterer, and band or DJ. Start shopping for attire.
- Six months out: send save-the-dates, arrange officiant and ceremony details,
  order invitations, and plan the honeymoon.
- Three months out: finalize the menu and cake, confirm the flowers and decor,
  and build the day-of timeline.
- One month out: send invitations early enough to collect RSVPs, confirm final
  headcount with the caterer, and complete any marriage license paperwork.
- One week out: confirm arrival times with every vendor, delegate day-of tasks,
  and assemble payments and gratuities in labeled envelopes.

Working backward from the date in this order prevents last-minute scrambling and
keeps deposits and deadlines from colliding.
