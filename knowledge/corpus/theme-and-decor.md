# Wedding theme, style, and decor

A cohesive theme ties the whole celebration together and makes decor decisions
easier. Start by choosing a color palette of two or three main colors plus a
neutral, then let it guide flowers, linens, stationery, attire accents, and
lighting. A consistent palette photographs beautifully and prevents a cluttered,
mismatched look.

Anchor the style to the venue rather than fighting it. A garden or barn suits a
relaxed, rustic look with greenery and string lights; a ballroom suits a formal,
classic style with candles and structured floral centerpieces; a modern loft
suits clean lines, monochrome accents, and minimal arrangements. Working with the
space costs less and looks more intentional than transforming it.

Focus decor budget on the areas guests spend the most time and photos happen: the
ceremony backdrop, the reception centerpieces, and a statement piece such as an
arch or a lounge area. Lighting is one of the highest-impact, lowest-cost
upgrades: warm string lights, uplighting, or candles change the entire mood of a
room for relatively little money.
