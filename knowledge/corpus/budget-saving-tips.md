# Ways to save money on a wedding

You can lower wedding costs substantially without making the day feel cheap. The
largest savings usually come from the date and the guest count. Choosing an
off-peak season, a Friday or Sunday, or a morning or brunch reception can cut
venue and catering prices meaningfully. Trimming the guest list is the most
direct lever of all, because most per-person costs (meals, drinks, rentals,
favors, invitations) scale with headcount.

Prioritize spending on the few things you will remember most and economize on
the rest. Many couples splurge on photography and food while saving on flowers
by using seasonal, locally available blooms and reusing ceremony arrangements at
the reception. Digital invitations and RSVP tracking reduce stationery and
postage costs.

Ask each vendor what is included and what is billed separately, such as service
charges, overtime, cake-cutting fees, corkage, and delivery. Comparing all-in
quotes rather than headline prices prevents surprises and helps you find the best
overall value.
