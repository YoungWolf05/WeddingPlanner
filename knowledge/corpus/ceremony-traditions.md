# Ceremony traditions and cultural customs

Wedding ceremonies carry a rich variety of traditions, and many couples blend
customs from their families and cultures into one meaningful service. Common
symbolic rituals include exchanging vows and rings, a unity ceremony such as
lighting a unity candle or blending two colors of sand, and a first kiss to close
the ceremony.

Cultural customs add depth and personal significance. Examples include a tea
ceremony honoring parents and elders, a handfasting in which the couple's hands
are gently bound with cloth or cord to symbolize their union, breaking a glass to
mark the moment of celebration, a garland or lei exchange, and jumping the broom.
Choose the elements that reflect who you are, and if you are combining
traditions, decide the order together so the ceremony flows naturally.

Discuss your plans with your officiant early. They can help sequence readings,
rituals, and vows, advise on any requirements if the ceremony is religious, and
make sure everyone involved, from readers to ring bearers, knows their cue on the
day.
